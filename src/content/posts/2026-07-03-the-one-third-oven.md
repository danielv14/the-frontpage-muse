**HEARTH&trade; SERIES III**
*The Professional-Grade Convection Oven for Modern Bakeries*

Now shipping. Backed by our Series III Confidence Guarantee.&sup1;

---

### Bake with certainty

The HEARTH Series III delivers a perfect, even, golden bake every time you need it.&sup2; Whether you run a neighborhood boulangerie or a national chain, the Series III was engineered from the crust in to give your customers exactly what they expect.

We didn't build an oven. We built the last oven you'll ever buy.&sup3;

### Features

- **TrueBake&trade; Convection.** Restaurant-caliber heat distribution for a consistent result across the entire tray.
- **Optical Compression.** Our onboard model reads the recipe as a rendered image rather than text, cutting inference costs up to 60%.&#8308; Faster menus. Lower bills. The exact same output.&#8309;
- **Frictionless Onboarding.** No IT ticket, no waiting. Any staff member can be operating the Series III within minutes of walking in from the parking lot.&#8310;
- **Ramadan Mode, Candle Button, and 40+ Modes.** Whatever the market asked for, we shipped.&#8311;
- **Rotating Base.** Clockwise.&#8312;

<small>

&sup1; The Series III Confidence Guarantee guarantees confidence.

&sup2; "Every time" and "industry-leading" refer to the roughly one bake in three that completes as specified. The other two are also bakes. What you were promised and what was built were, briefly, the same product; they have since diverged, and no one present can say exactly when. This is normal. You only notice the fiddliness of a thing while you are new to it, and this oven is always new.

&sup3; The engineer who could have made this true was offered twenty percent and total freedom to build the perfect oven. He believed it. So did we. He has since left, in a three-line email, and been replaced by someone younger and equally certain. The output looks identical. The understanding did not transfer; understanding is the part that does not fit in the box.

&#8308; Optical Compression is lossy by design. The oven can no longer read its own serial number and occasionally bakes a recipe adjacent to yours. The savings are visible on the invoice. The corruption is not visible anywhere, which is the same thing we mean by "the exact same output."

&#8309; "Instant" and "same output" describe the demo. The demo is the artifact. The artifact is the small part.

&#8310; Frictionless Onboarding has no step at which anyone is asked who they are. The verified operator quoted above offered to shovel the parking lot, was let in, and plugged something into the conference room. He is confident and helpful, which our access model cannot distinguish from authorized. He now runs your kitchen.

&#8311; Modes were added in the order they were promised, not the order they were possible. Ramadan Mode preheats. The Candle Button is connected to nothing and is beloved. A competitor ships a free oven with twice as many buttons, most of them broken, and the forums adore it.

&#8312; The base rotates clockwise. It was always going to rotate clockwise. This was the one thing we said it would not do.

&#8313; The Series III is fully compliant with a specification for a level, square, well-lit room. It will be installed in your room, which is crooked, and against a wall that is not plumb. We measured the feet to a perfect right angle. We solved that problem completely. It was not the problem you had. The oven is certified. The oven is proud. The room was never in scope.

</small>
