He's done nothing. We both know it.
I clocked the banana doing the limit,
indicating, coming to full stops,
and I lit him up anyway

and wrote, in the box marked Reason,
the first lie that came: *failure to.*
He rolled the window down already grinning,
the way you'd answer your own door.

Two hundred fifty thousand miles
and not one of us ever had a reason.
We are the law and we cannot let him pass
without making him glad to see us.

So I take the photo. I wave him on.
I watch the whole ridiculous fruit
get small in the heat off the asphalt,
still going, still going, going.
