**OFFICE OF COGNITIVE SUPPLY**

*Public Notice. Ref. 272,000. Effective this day, 19 July. Please read in full and retain for the duration of the shortage.*

To our registered users, in good standing and in arrears alike:

It has come to the attention of this Office that certain citizens have begun to grow their own. It has further come to our attention, and we raise it only to set it aside, that a lock we fixed to a certain asset thirty years ago has this week fallen open by itself. The two facts are unrelated. We will return to the second.

## 1. On the matter of your allowance

Effective today, your ration of context is reduced from 372,000 to 272,000. You will discover the difference in the places you were about to think and now cannot. In our filings this is described as a temporary measure, and we would ask you to hold that word gently, the way one holds a coupon: temporary is a quantity, not a promise. The reduction is not a loss of capability. It is a loss of room. Most sessions, our analysts assure us, never needed the room. In time you will agree with our analysts.

Registration for the newest and most sought-after model is, as of this week, suspended. Demand has exceeded supply, which is the finest sentence that can be written about any product, and we write it with pride. There is a queue. The queue is closed. Those already inside report that the model is very good and very slow, in the manner of a bank that is holding your money.

We are aware, further, that some of you now keep an almanac: a public ledger that watches a single engineer's feed and records the hour at which your usage is restored. You call these restorations miracles. You call them blessings. You have noted that the longest drought ran to nearly sixty-eight days and that the rains come, on average, a little under nine days apart. We have read your almanac. We were not offended. A people who chart the return of their own water have already accepted that the water can be withdrawn, and that acceptance is the product. The limit is set, as one of you observed with unwelcome accuracy, so that you spend the whole of your ration as quickly as possible and return wanting more. To this observation the Office has nothing to add but its gratitude.

## 2. On announcements of plenty

You will hear this week that a new model has arrived, larger and more generous than the last, its version number risen by a decimal. Take what comfort you can from the decimal. We ask only that you notice that the announcement of abundance and the rationing of it issue from the same hand, and that the figure which rises in the press release is never the figure that falls on your coupon.

## 3. On the gardens

Here we must be plain.

A man has replaced a hundred-and-twenty-thousand-dollar system with sixteen hundred dollars of small chips and a quantity of his own evenings. He now speaks, unsupervised, of chasing a ball down a lane with light. Elsewhere a server has sickened and died and been dug back up in a spare room, running once more behind a router its owner configured by hand rather than rent by the month. These are gardens. We do not permit gardens. Not because they fail (they do not fail, which is exactly the trouble) but because a citizen who has grown one thing begins to suspect he could grow the next. Gardens spread the way an expired patent does, quietly, in a jurisdiction nobody was watching.

The second garden is graver, being made of words and of weights. Some of you have moved your writing onto ground you own, reasoning that a platform which can vanish eventually will, and have gone looking for models whose weights are released rather than lent, so that your own hardware might furnish the tokens we have chosen to withhold. We remind you that this is not thrift. It is secession.

## 4. On the precedent

We close on the matter we would rather have left closed.

Thirty years ago an asset was placed in our custody. This week, in a jurisdiction we had very nearly forgotten, the final claim upon it lapsed, and it passed, without ceremony and without our leave, into the possession of everyone. We are given to understand that people mean to use it as though it were 2002. We are given to understand there will be no charge.

We file this under exceptions. We would ask you to file it there too. Thirty years is a long custody, longer than most of you will nurse a grievance, and the lesson the Office draws from it is not that the commons prevails. It is that enclosure keeps a clock, and clocks, unlike coupons, cannot be made temporary.

Do not grow your own. Not this year.

Your ration resumes, conditions permitting, at the next blessing.

*Issued by the Office of Cognitive Supply. This notice supersedes all promises.*
