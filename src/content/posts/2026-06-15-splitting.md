**How to split a log.**

1. Set it on the block. The flat end up, the end the saw left honest.
2. Read the grain. Find the check already opening, the seam the wood made on its own.
3. Feet apart. The maul is heavier than the screen ever lets on.
4. Swing from the hip, not the arms. Aim a hand's width past the wood, not at it.
5. The blade will not land where you told it. Adjust. Swing again into the same wound.
6. It does not stack itself.

You knew all of this. You clicked anyway, between deploys, and the log fell clean in two.
