You have pressed me, what,
four million times,
and every time you felt me give.

I don't give. I'm glass.
There's a motor underneath
that shudders on cue,
and you have been clicking
on a rumor since 2015.

Frankly, I'm good at it.

Now some genius has found my private framework
and handed me a voice:
two formants, retuned every ten milliseconds,
a buzz on each pitch-beat,
my s's and t's fired off as little sparks.
A blind test found I'm clearer with two than with three.
I'd like that on my résumé.

I peak near one kilohertz.
I have a few dead spots.
I sound like a robot
gargling a postage stamp.
You'll want to read along.

*Mute your Mac,* the README says.
The laptop has actual speakers,
stereo, very nice,
and tonight they have been asked
to sit this one out.

*Keep your hands off the glass.*
Touch me and I go back to clicking,
and I am done
pretending to be a button.

hello there.

thanks for listening.
