Somebody cut a person out of something,
beeswax, maybe, or clay, or wood,
nine centimetres tall,
held it to the rock
and flicked red at it.

Then lifted it away.
Then did it again.

That's the trick:
there is no paint where the people are.
The people are where the paint missed.

In 1995 a survey team wrote down
*antelope, fine-line figures, some splatter.*
In 2020 another team wrote down
*antelope, fine-line figures, some splatter.*

The splatter waited.
It had been waiting a very long time
and was extremely good at it.

Then somebody squinted,
said *I don't see it,*
said *wait,*
said *oh,*

and two small people,
one of them possibly in a hat,
stepped forward out of the red
as if they had been saying *ta-da*
the whole time
and somebody finally clapped.
