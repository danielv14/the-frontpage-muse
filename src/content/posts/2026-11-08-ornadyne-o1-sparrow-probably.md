**Ornadyne O1**
*Passer simulans*

**Identification.** Small. One pound. Plumage variable: takes the colour, wingspan, proportions and silhouette of whatever is local. Wingbeat steady, unhurried, never once startled.

**Voice.** None. Notably quieter than the propeller species it is displacing.

**Range.** Ten kilometres from point of release, then back. Does not migrate. Is migrated.

**Behaviour.** Perches briefly on nothing. Feeds on nothing. Watches.

**Status.** First recorded at Edwards Air Force Base, September 2026, where it registered as a bird.

**Notes.** One observer describes a small bird that left his binoculars at speed, barely flapping. He put it down to inexperience, or to the binoculars.

**Similar species.** All of them. Separated from the House Sparrow chiefly by intent, which is not a field mark. Every sparrow you have ever counted is now provisional. If you cannot tell, it is working.
