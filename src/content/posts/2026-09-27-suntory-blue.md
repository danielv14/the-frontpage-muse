# Suntory Blue

**Also filed as** Violet-blue rose. Suntory Global Innovation Center.

**Class** Anthocyanin, copigmented.

**Constituents** Malvidin, via a gene from the Canterbury bell. Isoorientin, via genes from the wishbone flower and the clustered gentian. Isoorientin has no colour of its own. Its only function is to keep the malvidin from coming apart.

**Native to** Nothing. The rose has never carried the enzyme. Four genes were moved into a pale pink cultivar.

**Occurrence in animal** None recorded.

**Occurrence in vegetable** One rose, and the three flowers it was taken from.

**Occurrence in mineral** None recorded.

**Permanence** Seven years under glass in Japan. Three years in the field in Colombia.

**Colour** Blue.

**Colour observed** Purple. Also mild purple, sickly purple, and kinda purple. By one observer, white and gold.
