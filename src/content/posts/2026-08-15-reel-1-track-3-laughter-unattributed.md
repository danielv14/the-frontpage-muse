**REEL 1: LAUGHTER, UNATTRIBUTED**

Stock: Magnetophon tape, ex-Signal Corps.
Source: house audience, recorded live.
Programme material: withdrawn. Not for air.

**TRACK 1 / 0:02**
Single, male, front row. Anticipatory. Use before the line, not after.

**TRACK 2 / 0:04**
Mixed, building. Clean tail. General purpose.

**TRACK 3 / 0:11**
Full house. Two women in the balcony carry it past the eighth second and have to be faded under. Best material on the reel. Do not lay over dialogue.

**TRACK 4 / 0:06**
As Track 3, second take. Thinner.

**TRACK 5 / 0:03**
Scattered. Glass drops at 0:02. Usable if trimmed to 0:02.

**TRACK 6 / 0:19**
Applause with laughter beneath it. Hold in reserve.

**NOTES**

Tracks 1 to 6 recorded the same evening, same house, same cause.

Cause not held on this reel. Refer to Programme Dept.

Programme Dept. holds no copy.

Duplicate before splicing. This is the only one.
