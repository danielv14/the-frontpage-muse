**508. Illustration, with intermittent animation.** A is the plate, engraved. B is the animation, not present. Motion is supplied by the observer, at whatever rate he can manage.

**509. Compound pulley, for the raising of heavy weights.** A and B are the ropes, which pass downward over both sheaves. The weight at C rises.

**510. A drives B.** The name of this movement was printed on the facing page, which is not reproduced.

**511.** A is the volume. B is the model. B receives A entire and returns it on request. No part is lettered between them.

**512. High torque at small displacement, from a small motor.** A is the motor. B is the gear-train, not determined. The workman was called away.

**513. Recurring escapement.** Circular motion from circular motion. A is the page; B, the same page five years since; C, eleven. The wheel is released at each interval and stands where it stood, ready.
