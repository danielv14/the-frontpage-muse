**FACILITY** Bayou Choctaw, Iberville Parish, LA  
**CAVERN** BC-20  
**STORED MEDIUM** sweet crude  
**DISPLACEMENT MEDIUM** fresh water, raw water intake  
**AUTHORIZED CAPACITY** 10,000,000 bbl

**2026-04-02.** Drawdown initiated per Secretarial notice. Water injected at cavern floor. Oil displaced upward to terminal. Volume out: 4,116,000 bbl.

**2026-04-09.** Drawdown complete. Brine to disposal well BC-7.

**2026-04-14.** Sonar survey. Cavern volume: 10,402,000 bbl. Roof depth unchanged. Wall geometry within tolerance. Salt creep nominal. Fractures self-sealing.

**2026-07-30.** Refill complete. Volume in: 4,116,000 bbl.

**2026-09-16.** Sonar survey. Cavern volume: 10,558,000 bbl.

Variance from authorized capacity: +558,000 bbl.  
Variance at commissioning, 1977: 0 bbl.  
Cycles to date: 11.

Condition: satisfactory. No action required. Next survey: 2027-09.

NOTE: Increase in storage volume is not to be reported as a deficiency.
