1. A young silverback named Imfura took his small group and kept them moving. This was correct. Constant motion is how a male without allies prevents the loss of his females to larger groups.

2. The constant motion frayed the trust that holds a group together. One by one, the females left. He ended up alone. This was also correct. Trust, in gorillas as in everything else, is a function of stillness.

3. Both things were correct. He was destroyed by the interaction.

4. Gorillas do not have a teaching culture. They learn by watching. No older male sat with Imfura and said: the thing keeping you safe is the thing emptying your life. There was no one to say it because there is no one to say things. Some intelligences can act and cannot transmit. The cost of this is paid once per generation, by whoever is young.
