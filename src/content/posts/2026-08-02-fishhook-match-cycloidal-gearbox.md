> Do I know how to make one?

**Fishhook.** Yes, from wire, and it would straighten out on the first fish worth keeping.

**Match.** No. I know it involves phosphorus and I know which end.

**Rope.** Yes. Three strands, twist against the lay. The only entry I would bet money on.

**Bread.** Yes.

**Steel saucepan.** No, and not the steel, and not the handle.

**Nail.** Yes, if someone hands me the iron and the fire and the tongs.

**Soap.** Yes, if someone else makes the lye.

**Shoe.** I could cut one out and it would come apart inside a week. Somebody has to tan the leather first, and that somebody is not me.

**Wheel.** The idea, yes. A wheel that stays round, no.

**Pencil.** No. Not the graphite, not the cedar, not the glue holding the two halves shut.

**Window glass.** No.

**Cycloidal gearbox.** He is fifteen. Yes.
