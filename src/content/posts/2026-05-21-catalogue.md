Of all the passages it could have been
it was the list.

Not Hector falling, not the shield,
not the old man kissing the hands.
Ajax, Nireus, Idomeneus,
twelve ships, eighty ships, forty ships,
captains whose only appearance in the poem
is their appearance in the poem.

A reader buried with a register.
Homer eulogizing footnotes.
The dirt indifferent to either.
