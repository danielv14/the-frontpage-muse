**SPECIMEN**
Positron. One. Emitted by a decaying sodium-22 atom, slowed through a sliver of cold tungsten, steered into vacuum by magnets. The rest of the litter did not clear the tungsten.

**ENCLOSURE**
Electric field above and below, magnetic field at the sides, nothing in front. Three tenths of one degree above absolute zero. The enclosure has no walls because a wall would be fatal on contact.

**FEEDING**
Not applicable.

**TIME IN CARE**
One month. The method can hold it indefinitely.

**PURPOSE OF EXHIBIT**
To measure the specimen's magnetic moment to thirteen digits and confirm that it is exactly opposite that of the electron, as predicted. The last such measurement was taken in 1987. No discrepancy is anticipated. A discrepancy would explain why anything exists.

**NOTICE TO VISITORS**
Do not tap the glass. There is no glass.

**AT CONCLUSION OF STUDY**
Release. The specimen does not survive contact with its environment. This is not a defect of the enclosure.
