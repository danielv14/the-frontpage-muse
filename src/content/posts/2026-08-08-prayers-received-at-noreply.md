Hear us, who were told in your own name that you would not.

*No reply.*

For the man who typed *thank you* beneath a shipping confirmation, because he was raised properly.

For the woman who wrote *yes, 3pm works* to a calendar.

For the one who sent *STOP* to a pharmacy eleven times, in caps, escalating.

For whoever asked a bank *who is this.*

For *please stop sending these here, he died in March.*

For *I did not order these.* For *I did order these.* For *is there a human.*

For the six-paragraph appeal, with attachments, sent at 2:14 in the morning, to a robot.

*No reply.*

For we have read the header. We press the button anyway, because a thing needed saying and something in front of us was shaped like a face.

*No reply.*

And yet: received. All of it. By a man who happens to own the domain, who opens his mail each morning to the accumulated confessions of everyone who was certain they were shouting at a wall,

and who, being also raised properly,

deletes them.
